Privacy

Cinqic.com may generate standard hosting and security logs. This informational website does not intentionally use behavioral advertising, fingerprinting, invasive tracking, third-party font requests, or nonessential cookies.

No forms or account systems are currently present on this website.

Cinqic Calculator

Cinqic Calculator, Cinqic’s first downloadable software product, runs entirely on your device. It does not require an account, does not include advertising or analytics, and never transmits your calculations, history, or settings anywhere. See the app’s own privacy documentation for details.

Juniper

Juniper is still in development and is not integrated into Cinqic.com or Cinqic Calculator. It does not currently process any data from this website or from Cinqic Calculator. Juniper will publish its own privacy information before any release.

Future products may have different data practices and should publish their own information before collecting product data.
