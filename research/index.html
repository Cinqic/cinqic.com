01 — Active project

Juniper Auto

One small, open, sparse Mixture-of-Experts language model research project exploring whether a single approximately 150-million-parameter model can become capable, local, customizable, and autonomous on consumer hardware.

Phase 3 candidate pending independent review

Architecture and tokenizer work are in progress.

Phases 0, 1, and 2 are approved; Phase 3 has produced a candidate unified byte-level BPE tokenizer. No model has been trained on real data, and no trained checkpoint, autonomy runtime, tools, or demonstrated expert specialization exists.

02 — Research record

What the phases mean right now.

Phase 0

Approved

Foundation, governance, frozen configuration, reproducibility, and recovery.

Phase 1

Approved

Executable sparse and dense reference architectures plus training plumbing.

Phase 2

Independently approved

Routing, dispatch, diagnostics, ablations, and context-sensitivity infrastructure.

Phase 3

Candidate

Unified tokenizer candidate pending independent review; tokenizer work only.

Not a product release: Juniper Auto has no trained consumer model or downloadable runtime. The canonical repository is the source of truth.

03 — Completed research

Juniper Math 1

Juniper Math 1 is a completed and retired research project. Its final conclusion found that the tested small architecture and training regimes learned meaningful component behaviors, but no Phase 8 checkpoint simultaneously met the project’s direct capability, tool-use, retention, and reliability gates.

Historical record

It is not an active model project, and no Phase 8 production or release checkpoint was approved.
