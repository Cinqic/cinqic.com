About Cinqic

Cinqic is a software and artificial intelligence company being built by Markus Gillyard.

Mission

Build software that helps people learn, create, think clearly, and solve problems while respecting user privacy and ownership.

Vision

Create an easily accessible AI ecosystem that is useful, affordable, and trustworthy for everyone.

Founder

Markus Gillyard founded Cinqic to create practical, accessible software and local-first AI that respects user privacy, ownership, and control.

Current focus

Cinqic’s current focus is the development and future release of Juniper Baby 1. Education, automation, productivity, robotics, and consumer hardware remain future possibilities, not current product divisions.
