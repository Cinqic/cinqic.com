Cinqic Calculator

A lightweight, private desktop calculator for Windows. Perform standard and scientific calculations, convert common units, review local calculation history, and use practical financial tools — without an account, cloud service, or internet connection.

Download for Windows ↓ View source

Windows 10/11 · 64-bit · Version 1.0.0

01 — Overview

Useful without an account, and without AI.

Cinqic Calculator is Cinqic’s first small public software product — a genuinely useful desktop tool built around the same principle that will guide the rest of Cinqic’s software: AI should remain a choice.

01

Standard & scientific

Arithmetic, percentages, square roots, powers, trigonometry, factorials, and more.

02

Unit conversion

Length, mass, temperature, area, volume, speed, time, and data storage — decimal and binary units kept distinct.

03

Financial tools

Discounts, sales tax, tips, bill splitting, and simple & compound interest estimates.

04

Local history

Recent calculations stay on your device, with an easy way to disable or clear them.

03 — Installation

Getting started

  1. Download the installer above and run it.
  2. Follow the setup prompts (no administrator privileges needed).
  3. Launch Cinqic Calculator from the Start menu.
  4. Use it fully offline — no account or internet connection required.

04 — Privacy

Works fully offline

Cinqic Calculator does not require an account, does not include advertising or analytics, and never transmits your calculations, history, or settings anywhere. Settings and optional local history stay on your device. See the app’s privacy documentation for details.

Juniper relationship

Built for the future Juniper ecosystem.

Cinqic Calculator works entirely without artificial intelligence. Juniper is not integrated into Cinqic Calculator 1.0. Future versions may add optional local Juniper explanations while the calculator keeps working fully offline without AI. AI should remain a choice.
