Juniper Auto

One small, open, sparse Mixture-of-Experts language model.

An open, consumer-scale research project exploring whether one approximately 150M-parameter sparse Mixture-of-Experts language model can become a capable, local, customizable autonomous intelligence system — not a collection of separate specialist models.

Phase 0 foundation approved · in development
