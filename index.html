Cinqic

Software that works for the user

Cinqic is an artificial intelligence and software company being built by Markus Gillyard. Its mission is to build helpful open-source software that supports learning, creativity, clear thinking, and problem-solving while respecting user privacy and ownership.

Cinqic focuses on practical technology rather than unnecessary complexity. Its software is intended to remain understandable, adaptable, and useful without forcing people into permanent dependence on cloud services.

Future flagship product

Meet Juniper

Juniper is a lightweight, local-first AI assistant in development. It is planned to run locally whenever practical, remain useful without unnecessary cloud dependence, and give users greater control over how their information is stored and used.

The current research goal is Juniper 1, a dense decoder-only language model with approximately 20 million parameters, designed for fast, efficient, and helpful text conversation. This is a development target, not a finished capability.

AI should not require surrendering control

Private by default

Whenever practical, user information should remain on hardware the user controls.

Useful offline

Core functions should continue working without a permanent internet connection whenever technically possible.

Modifiable

Users and developers should be able to inspect, adapt, and extend open-source components.

Efficient

Juniper should prioritize practical performance and low hardware requirements rather than chasing size for its own sake.

Cinqic product policy

AI is an option, not a requirement

Cinqic products are designed to be useful on their own without AI. Juniper may enhance those products, but no Cinqic product should depend on AI to perform its essential functions.

Users are free to use as much or as little AI as they choose.

Principles

  • Truth over confidence
  • Education first
  • Privacy by default
  • Simplicity over complexity
  • Practical innovation
  • Continuous improvement
  • Safety as a permanent priority

Independent AI

The philosophy guiding Juniper’s development

Independent AI was inspired in part by Anthropic’s Constitutional AI methodology, but it is a separate philosophy with its own goals, priorities, and principles. It is intended to guide Juniper toward being useful, private, honest, practical, transparent, non-evasive, grounded in explicit principles, and respectful of user control.

  1. Responses are drafted.
  2. Responses are evaluated against explicit principles.
  3. Weak or misleading answers are critiqued and revised.
  4. Improved examples may be used for supervised fine-tuning.
  5. Preference data may be used in later training stages.
  6. The model is repeatedly evaluated for honesty, usefulness, safety, and clarity.

The goal is not to imitate big-technology assistants. The goal is to build something honest, flexible, efficient, and more aligned with the person using it.

Independent AI was inspired in part by ideas introduced through Anthropic’s Constitutional AI research. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

What Cinqic is working toward

  • Build a helpful local AI assistant.
  • Make AI more accessible.
  • Keep costs low while maintaining quality.
  • Reduce dependence on cloud AI whenever practical.
  • Create AI that teaches instead of merely producing answers.
  • Develop specialized AI agents that work together through Juniper.

One interface. Specialized intelligence.

The long-term vision is for Juniper to become an open-source ecosystem of specialized AI agents that work together through one simple interface. These agents would help users learn, create, automate tasks, and solve problems while maintaining ownership and control of their data.
