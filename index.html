Mission

Software should help people without taking control away from them.

Cinqic is building toward accessible, affordable tools that support learning, creativity, clear thinking, and problem solving. Privacy and ownership are product concerns, not fine print.

Learn with clarity

Tools should help people understand and make decisions, not create blind dependency.

Create with ownership

Improve with care

Practical innovation means evaluating what works, admitting limits, and improving deliberately.

Flagship product

Meet Juniper

Juniper is Cinqic’s planned local-first AI assistant, designed to help users learn, create, automate, and solve problems through one clear interface. Over time, it may coordinate specialized agents while keeping user control central.

Company principle

AI is an option, not a requirement.

Cinqic products are designed to remain useful on their own without requiring artificial intelligence. Juniper may enhance the ecosystem, but people should control whether AI participates in their work.

Useful without AI

Basic product functionality should remain useful.

User-controlled participation

People should choose as much or as little AI as they want.

Clear processing disclosure

Local and cloud processing should be explained when used.
