Juniper Math 1

A purpose-built mathematical reasoning and tool-use small language model.

Juniper Math 1 is Cinqic’s standalone mathematical specialist and the first active specialist being developed for the broader Juniper architecture. It is designed to interpret math problems, route appropriate work to deterministic tools, verify results, and answer honestly.

Learn more

The research question is how capable, reliable, efficient, and truthfully calibrated an approximately five-million-parameter mathematical language model can become when its architecture, tokenizer, tools, training, and evaluation are optimized around one domain. No trained model or Cinqic Calculator integration exists yet.

Juniper Math 1

Accuracy over breadth.

The project is deliberately narrow: it explores mathematical interpretation, decomposition, tool selection, verification, ambiguity detection, and truthful uncertainty handling rather than presenting a generic chatbot.

01

Understand

Interpret ordinary mathematical language, notation, and the information a problem provides.

02

Route

Choose structured operations and a deterministic tool when exact computation is appropriate.

03

Verify

Check tool results and avoid presenting unsupported or fabricated mathematical answers.

04

Stay honest

Recognize ambiguity, missing information, and requests outside the validated scope.
