Juniper 1 20M

A compact foundation for a larger ecosystem.

Juniper 1 20M is an early foundation for Juniper, a future lightweight, local-first AI assistant — not the complete assistant or ecosystem.

Learn more

Juniper 1 20M is a decoder-only language model with roughly 20 million parameters, developed from scratch as a lightweight foundation for fast, efficient conversation. It is intended as the first step toward a broader open-source Juniper ecosystem, guided by Cinqic’s Independent AI principles: truth over confidence, privacy by default, and user control.
