Transparency

Transparency is a product feature: claims should be clear, limited, and supported.

Cinqic intends to publish model origin, foundation-model attribution, licenses, training and fine-tuning summaries where appropriate, evaluations, limitations, system requirements, release hashes, release notes, and local/remote processing disclosures with a public release.

Current commitments

  • Separate measured performance from marketing language.
  • Publish known limitations and correct inaccurate claims.
  • Preserve source-model attribution.
  • Explain what runs locally and what uses remote services.
  • Avoid calling experiments finished products.
  • Avoid privacy claims the architecture cannot guarantee.
